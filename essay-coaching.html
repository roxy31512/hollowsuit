Important information

This applies to both fellowship and essay services.

What I do and don’t do
I do not write, rewrite, or ghostwrite. My role is to help you develop your ideas, structure, and argument. I will not write sentences, edit grammar or spelling, or format bibliographies and citations.
No guaranteed outcomes
Application outcomes, admissions, and grading decisions cannot be guaranteed. My coaching helps you present your strongest work, but all writing, original ideas, and final submissions remain your sole responsibility.
Check your institution’s rules
Rules on outside help differ by course, instructor, and program. It's your responsibility to confirm that coaching is allowed for your assignment, or to check with your supervisor, before booking.
Drafts and word limits
Drafts must be submitted at least 72 hours before the scheduled call. If it isn't sent in time, the call is rescheduled or converted to an Option A strategy session without an async review. Word limits apply to body text only. For fellowship reviews, the limit is 2,000 words and I stop reading at the limit. For essays, pricing follows the word-count tiers above.
Scope of each service
Each service ends at the scope listed. I don't offer final polishing, proofreading, or line editing. Any further async review or call after a service is complete is booked and paid for separately.
Payment, rescheduling and cancellations
Full payment is required at the time of booking to reserve your spot. Because I take only a small number of clients each week to give each person dedicated attention, refunds are not provided for change of mind, late cancellations, or missed sessions once a booking is confirmed.
You may reschedule a session once, free of charge, with at least 24 hours’ notice. The new session must take place (not just be booked) within 30 days of the original session date. Any further change is treated as a late cancellation. For sessions with a draft review, the review is carried over at no extra cost, and the draft is due 72 hours before the new session date.
Sessions cancelled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours’ notice, or where you arrive more than 15 minutes late, are forfeited. If you arrive late, the session ends at its scheduled time. A rescheduled session not completed within the 30-day window is also forfeited. Notice is measured against the session time shown in the booking confirmation.
In the unlikely event that I need to cancel or move a session, you may choose between a full refund or a free reschedule at a time that works for you.
Nothing in this policy affects any statutory consumer rights that cannot be excluded under the laws of your country of residence.
