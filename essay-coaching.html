How would you like to work together?

Live sessions

A video call where we talk through your essay or thesis chapter together, in real time. One-on-one or in a small group.

I don't read your draft beforehand. I ask questions and point out issues, and you decide on and make every change.

Written feedback

Send your draft and assignment by email. I return it with comments and a written summary on argument, structure and clarity.

Comments are questions and pointers, not rewrites. Turnaround depends on length.

Good to know

I teach and give feedback. I don't write or rewrite sentences, supply thesis statements, arguments or sources, or make changes to your draft, and I can't guarantee specific grades.

Rules on outside help differ by course and instructor. It's your responsibility to confirm it's allowed for your assignment, or with your supervisor, before booking.
